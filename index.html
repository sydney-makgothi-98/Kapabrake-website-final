Solving Real Problems

Built for the Harshest Conditions on Earth

Mining and heavy industry present engineering challenges that off-the-shelf solutions cannot solve. Extreme heat, constant vibration, corrosive environments, and enormous loads demand systems that are engineered from the ground up for durability.

Kapabrake's engineering philosophy is simple: design for worst-case scenarios, then test beyond them. All brake lining that we ship has been subjected to conditions more extreme than it will ever face in the field.

Product Offering

Woven Braking Products
Built for Extreme Duty

From standard brake lining to custom-shaped components, our products are engineered to deliver consistent friction performance, thermal stability, and ease of fitment.

Woven Lining

Available in 10m rolls or flat sheets, with thicknesses ranging from 6.4mm to 25mm. Other thicknesses available on request.

Custom Shapes

Cut-to-size linings, shaped shoes and bands, bonded assemblies, and drilled mounting surfaces for a perfect application-specific fit.

Material Versions

Available in a variety of KB (KapaBrake) versions.

Fitment & Performance

Our 3-axis weave and brass wire reinforcement provide wear resistance, heat conduction, and stable friction values. Surfaces are supplied ground for reliable bonding or riveting.

Thermal Conditioning

Flexible cold-fit lining can be heat-set at 160°C for exact moulding. We also cure linings to shoes and bands to ensure the best possible fitment.

Refurbishment Services

Brake removal, sandblasting, coating, and relining services are handled in-house with our own blast, paint, and curing facilities.
