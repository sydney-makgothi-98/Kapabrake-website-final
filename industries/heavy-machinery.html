The Heavy Industry Challenge

When Brakes Run
Around the Clock

Heavy industry operates continuously. Braking systems must maintain performance through millions of duty cycles without unplanned downtime. Kapabrake designs for exactly this reality.

Large processing plants — steel mills, cement plants, paper mills — operate their drive machinery continuously for months between planned maintenance windows. Brake systems must maintain specified braking torque throughout, with predictable wear rates that allow planned pad replacement during scheduled shutdowns.

Earth-moving and construction equipment operates in environments with high vibration, constant shock loading, and extreme dust contamination. Our sealed brake lining for mobile heavy equipment are designed for these conditions, with robust mounting systems that absorb vibration without transmitting it to brake components. Proper density ensures good engagement on earthmoving equipment and large-scale machinery.

Our Solutions

Heavy Machinery Brake Range

01

Mill & Kiln Brakes

High-torque holding and stopping brakes for rotating mills, kilns, and dryers in cement, mineral processing, and bulk material plants. Designed for low-speed, high-torque continuous duty applications.

02

Rolling Mill Brakes

Fast-acting emergency stop brakes for steel and aluminium rolling mills. Sub-second stopping times, high thermal capacity, and integration with mill emergency stop systems.

03

Earth-Moving Equipment

Service and parking brakes for large excavators, bulldozers, and mining trucks. Sealed against dust and moisture, vibration-resistant mounting, and rated for steep slope holding.

04

Wind Turbine Brakes

Yaw and rotor lock brakes for utility-scale wind turbines. Corrosion-resistant for coastal and offshore wind farm environments. Designed to IEC 61400 standards.

05

Power Generation

Turning gear, shaft braking, and emergency stop systems for thermal power plant machinery. Integration with plant DCS and emergency shutdown systems.

06

Material Handling

Brake systems for bucket wheel reclaimers, stacker-reclaimers, and blending bed equipment at bulk material terminals and coal handling facilities.
