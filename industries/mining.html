Mining Brake Lining

Engineered for the deepest shafts and harshest surface conditions — Kapabrake's mining brake lining meet the exacting demands of modern mine operations, from hoist safety to conveyor control.

Certified and Tested for exacting Mine Winder conditions, Kapabrake offers a fully customised woven brake pad or lining.

Kapabrake's award winning mine winder brakes have been tried and tested for South Africa's deep level mining conditions.

"Kapabrake ...Are best in class in terms of the balance between fade resistance, friction recovery and heat dissipation, which translates into effective stopping power." — Independent Brake Analyst.

Kapabrake's adherence to ISO9001 systems include its own testing facilities to ensure each batch produced conforms to performance parameters.

Kapabrake offers superior performance at competitive prices with rapid delivery lead times.

Kapabrake has partnered with Winder Controls to supply and distribute premium woven brake lining products specifically developed for heavy-duty mining applications. Through this collaboration, customers benefit from Kapabrake's specialised brake lining products together with Winder Controls' extensive mine winder engineering expertise, technical support, and established service capabilities as a member of the SIEMAG TECBERG Group.
