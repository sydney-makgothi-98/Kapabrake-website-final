The Lifting Challenge

Precision Braking
Under Load

Crane and hoist braking is not about slowing motion — it is about holding loads with absolute certainty and stopping them with controlled precision. There is no margin for error.

Lifting applications require brake systems that hold static loads indefinitely without creep — any drift under a suspended load is unacceptable. Kapabrake's thrustor-applied disc brake systems are designed for zero-drift load holding, with independent monitoring confirming engagement before load is accepted. Even thickness ensures maximum mating on the drum face of cranes, elevators and lifting equipment.

Dynamic stopping — slowing a moving load to rest — requires carefully calibrated deceleration to avoid load swing, rope stress, or structural shock loading. Our electrohydraulic thrustor brakes can be tuned for application-specific deceleration profiles that protect both loads and crane structures.

Engineered Durability

Built for Continuous Operation

Heavy industry operates continuously. Brake lining must maintain performance through millions of duty cycles without unplanned downtime. Kapabrake designs for exactly this reality.

Earth-moving and construction equipment operates in environments with high vibration, constant shock loading, and extreme dust contamination. Our hard-wearing woven brake lining bonded with our specific protocols is a valuable part of civil site work. Surface preparation for the lining to match the actual drum shape means fast engagement on site without long delays to bedding-in on earthmoving equipment and large-scale machinery.
