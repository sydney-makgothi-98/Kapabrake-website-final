INDUSTRIAL

Friction Discs

Keep your heavy factory machines running safe and smooth with Kapabrake's tough woven brake lining. It stops heavy loads fast, handles extreme heat, and fits industrial clutches and brakes with ease.

Key Benefits

  • • High Heat Resistance: Keeps its grip even when machines run hot during long shifts. The inclusion of multiple strands of brass wire disperse heat to sides and edges even under extreme loads.
  • • Strong and Flexible: Bends to fit different sizes without breaking or cracking. Kapabrake can reline your existing brake bands or shoes and form the lining to the exact shape required.
  • • Long-Lasting Grip: Low wear means fewer replacements and less factory downtime.
  • • Smooth Control: Stops heavy industrial parts safely without sudden jerks (judder).
